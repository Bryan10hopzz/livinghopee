About Us

Giving Hope, Building Lives, Transforming Futures.

Our Journey

Living Hope Children’s Ministry began in 2010 with a heartfelt desire to reach out to poor, needy, vulnerable and orphaned children who were struggling without adequate care, support and opportunities. This initiative was started with the valuable help and commitment of a devoted servant of God named Anna, whose compassion for underprivileged children became an inspiration for this ministry.

Our initial work focused on identifying children from economically disadvantaged families, including orphaned and vulnerable children, and extending practical assistance to help them continue their education. We remained committed to supporting these children throughout their school years, encouraging and assisting them until they completed their schooling and progressed to college. It was our joy to see these children move forward in their education and pursue a better future.

Extending Our Mission to Odisha

As our concern for disadvantaged children grew, we extended our charitable ministry to the state of Odisha, India, where the needs of vulnerable children are particularly great.

In many communities, children face extreme poverty, inadequate educational opportunities and a lack of basic necessities. Some are orphaned or semi-orphaned, while others come from families struggling to survive. Sadly, there are children who even go without sufficient food and other essential resources.

Moved by the difficult circumstances of these children, we began our outreach in Golamunda remote village, Titlagarh, Odisha. Subsequently, we extended our support to children in Palsimila remote Village, Raikia, Odisha, reaching out to more children and families in need.

Our desire is not merely to offer temporary assistance but to bring encouragement, hope and opportunities into the lives of children who are often forgotten by society. We believe that every child, regardless of their family background or economic circumstances, deserves the opportunity to grow, learn and live with dignity.

Our Present Challenges and Needs

Although we have a strong desire to serve these children, our ministry currently faces a significant challenge: the lack of consistent financial and material support.

Because of our limited resources, we are unable to provide regular assistance to all the children who need our help. Our visits to these communities are therefore occasional rather than frequent. Whenever we are able to visit, we make every effort to provide essential resources according to the immediate needs of the children.

We sincerely wish to do much more. Our hearts are burdened by the needs we see, and we believe that with the support of compassionate individuals, churches, organizations and friends, we can extend our reach and provide more consistent assistance.

Our Vision for the Future

Our dream is to establish a more consistent and sustainable support system for vulnerable children, particularly in the underserved communities of Odisha. We hope to:

  • Provide regular educational assistance, including notebooks, school supplies and other learning materials.
  • Extend support for food, clothing and other essential needs.
  • Reach more orphaned, semi-orphaned and economically disadvantaged children.
  • Encourage children to continue their education and pursue their dreams.
  • Build meaningful relationships with children and their families through regular visits and compassionate care.
  • Develop partnerships with individuals, churches and organizations that share our concern for disadvantaged children.

We believe that a little help, when offered with genuine love and commitment, can make a lasting difference in a child’s life.

Activities & Projects

Extending Love, Sharing Hope, Transforming Lives

At Living Hope Children’s Ministry, we believe that every child deserves love, care, dignity and an opportunity for a better future. Inspired by the compassion of Jesus Christ, we reach out to underprivileged, orphaned and semi-orphaned children by extending practical assistance and encouragement according to their needs and the resources available to us.

Our ministry focuses on four important areas of children’s welfare. Although we have been reaching out to children and providing assistance, our activities are currently limited and cannot be carried out on a regular basis due to a lack of sufficient financial and material support. Nevertheless, our concern for these children remains strong, and we desire to do much more for them.

1

Children’s Education

Helping children learn, grow and pursue their dreams.

Education provides children with hope and opportunities for a brighter future. However, many children from economically disadvantaged families struggle to continue their education because they cannot afford even basic learning materials.

We extend educational assistance by providing notebooks, stationery, school supplies and other essential learning resources whenever we have the necessary support. Our desire is to encourage children to continue their education, complete their schooling and pursue their dreams without being discouraged by poverty.

With the support of committed ministry partners, we hope to extend this assistance to more children and provide it more consistently throughout their educational journey.

2

Clothing Assistance

Sharing kindness and restoring dignity.

Many children from poor and vulnerable families lack sufficient clothing and other basic necessities. Through our outreach visits, we provide clothing assistance to children according to their needs and the resources available.

A simple gift of clothing can bring happiness to a child and remind them that they are loved, valued and remembered.

We hope to reach more children with this practical expression of compassion through the partnership and generosity of caring individuals and organizations.

3

Food and Nutrition

Because no child should have to go hungry.

One of the most heartbreaking realities we encounter, particularly in some of the communities we visit in Odisha, is the extreme poverty experienced by children and their families. Some children struggle even to receive sufficient food for their daily needs.

Whenever resources are available, we provide food and essential provisions to children and families experiencing hardship. However, our present resources do not allow us to offer regular food assistance.

Our heartfelt desire is to develop a stronger support network through which we can respond more consistently to the nutritional needs of these vulnerable children.

4

Love, Care and Encouragement

Every child needs to know that someone cares.

Beyond material assistance, children need love, attention, encouragement and emotional support. Many orphaned and semi-orphaned children grow up facing difficult circumstances without adequate parental care and support.

During our visits, we spend time with the children, listen to them, encourage them and remind them that their lives are valuable and their future matters.

Although our visits are currently occasional because of limited resources, we remain committed to reaching these children whenever opportunities and support become available. We long to visit them more frequently, build meaningful relationships and provide continued encouragement.

An Urgent Need: Establishing Children’s Day Care Centers

Creating a Safe Place for Children to Learn, Grow and Flourish

One of our heartfelt dreams at Living Hope Children’s Ministry is to establish day care centers in needy communities, particularly in Palsimila remote village, Raikia, Odisha, India, where children are growing up in poverty, without adequate parental care, educational opportunities and basic necessities.

Many children in disadvantaged communities need more than occasional assistance. They need a safe and caring environment where they can receive regular attention, nutritious food, educational support, guidance and encouragement.

We envision establishing children’s day care centers that will provide a welcoming and supportive environment for underprivileged, orphaned and semi-orphaned children. These centers would serve as places where children can spend their time meaningfully, receive basic educational assistance, enjoy nutritious meals, participate in creative and developmental activities, and experience love and personal care.

Our desire is to make these centers places of hope where children are not forgotten, where their needs are recognized and where their potential is encouraged.

Our Vision for the Day Care Centers

Through these centers, we hope to:

  • Provide a safe and caring environment for vulnerable children.
  • Offer regular nutritious meals and basic nutritional support.
  • Provide educational assistance and opportunities for learning.
  • Encourage children’s physical, emotional, social and intellectual development.
  • Promote good values, discipline, confidence and positive relationships.
  • Extend consistent care and encouragement to children from disadvantaged families.

We Need Your Partnership

Establishing and maintaining day care centers requires suitable facilities, learning materials, food, staff and regular financial support. At present, our limited resources make it difficult to turn this vision into reality.

Therefore, we earnestly invite compassionate individuals, churches, Christian organizations and ministry partners to join hands with us in making this dream possible.

Your partnership can help us establish and sustain centers where children receive not merely temporary assistance but regular care, encouragement and opportunities for a better future. Together, let us create places where children are safe, loved, nourished, educated and encouraged to dream.

“Defend the weak and the fatherless; uphold the cause of the poor and the oppressed.”Psalm 82:3 (NIV)

A Day Care Center Today Can Build a Brighter Tomorrow.

Our Greatest Need: Committed Ministry Partners

We cannot do everything alone, but together we can make a greater difference.

Our desire to serve underprivileged children is greater than the resources currently available to us. We have witnessed the needs of these children, particularly in Palsimila remote village, Raikia in Odisha, and we know that much more needs to be done.

At present, our educational, clothing, food and care assistance depends largely on the availability of occasional support. We are therefore unable to provide consistent assistance or visit these communities as frequently as we would like.

This is why we earnestly seek committed ministry partners who share our concern for poor, orphaned, semi-orphaned and vulnerable children.

We invite individuals, families, churches, Christian organizations and compassionate friends to join hands with Living Hope Children’s Ministry. Your partnership can help us:

  • Provide more consistent educational assistance.
  • Extend food and clothing support to children in need.
  • Visit the children and communities more regularly.
  • Respond to the urgent needs of orphaned and semi-orphaned children.
  • Reach additional children living in poverty and difficult circumstances.

We are not simply looking for occasional contributions; we are prayerfully seeking committed partners who will stand with us in this mission and help make our ministry among children more sustainable.

Every contribution, whether large or small, can become a meaningful blessing in a child’s life.
